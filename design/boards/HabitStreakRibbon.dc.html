Habit Streak Ribbon

A compact, one-habit view of the current week: how long the streak is, what today needs, and what’s coming. Soft connectors carry the streak node to node — it is not a progress bar, a timeline, a ring or a heatmap.

USAGE
Use inPersonal Dashboard (habit widget) · Habits overview (one per habit, or the habit in focus) · Weekly Review → Goals & habits step, optional.
Never inTasks, projects, multi-habit comparison (that’s Habit Field), generic step indicators.
Week7 visible days. Locale decides the order: Sat → Fri for Iran (Persian or English UI), Mon → Sun for other locales.
